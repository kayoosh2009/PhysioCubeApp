PROCESS

How does the Physio Cube work?

01
Visit your nearest gym or sports center that cooperates with us.
02
Fill out a questionnaire about your injury (what happened, where it hurts, and what sport you play).
03
Get a set of exercises and perform them inside the Cube under the control of 3D cameras.
04
Submit your data to cloud processing and wait for AI analysis results.
05
Get a detailed report with a solution to your problem or a referral to a specialist.
